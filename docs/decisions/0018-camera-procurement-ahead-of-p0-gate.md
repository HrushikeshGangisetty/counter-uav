# 0018 — Five cameras procured ahead of the P0 bench gate (recorded deviation)

- **Date:** 2026-09-07
- **Status:** CLOSED — recorded as a **deviation from a stated working agreement**
- **Owner:** Person A (Hrushikesh)
- **Source:** `[Team 2026-09-07]`, `[PRD 4.1, 7.1]`, Document 4 §1.3

**Decision.** Five innomaker IMX296 units were purchased (2 for the pod, 3 spares)
**before** the P0 eight-hour bench go/no-go was run.

**Reasoning.** `[PRD 7.1]` states *"single-unit validation precedes bulk procurement,
without exception."* At five units the financial exposure is small, and the spares
hedge a supply risk the PRD itself names: Indian market availability *"has already
forced three camera changes"*, and `[PRD 4.1]` warns that *"waiting for a failure
before sourcing an alternative has already cost this project schedule once."*

**Trade-offs accepted.** It is nonetheless a deviation from a working agreement
written *"without exception"*. If the bench go/no-go fails, five units are stranded
rather than one. Recorded here rather than passing unnoticed, because the value of
the working agreement comes from noticing when it is bent.

**Alternatives considered.** Buy one, validate, then buy four — the letter of the
agreement, at the cost of a second lead time on a part with a demonstrated
availability problem.

**Unchanged.** The P0 bench go/no-go still runs and still gates M1. This entry
authorises no further bulk procurement: the **lens** decision (OD-19) remains gated on
measured figures, and OD-A1 (the six pass/fail thresholds) is still open.
